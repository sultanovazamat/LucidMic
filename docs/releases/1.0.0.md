# LucidMic 1.0.0

**Your voice. Minus the noise.**

Free, on-device AI noise removal for macOS. One switch in your menu bar, no account,
and no audio uploads.

## Install

Requires **Apple silicon (M1 or newer)** and **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**.

1. Download **LucidMic-1.0.0.dmg** below and drag LucidMic into Applications.
2. Open the app. It is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after trying to open it.
3. Turn **Noise removal** on from the menu bar, allow microphone access, and approve
   the one-time driver installation. Install before a call; the audio service restarts.

Apps using the system-default input receive cleaned audio automatically. If you
chose a specific microphone in your call app, select **LucidMic Microphone** there.

## Included

- Local DPDFNet2 noise removal at 48 kHz.
- Instant cleaning/bypass switch; audio keeps flowing in both modes.
- Bundled virtual microphone, launch at login, and in-app driver removal.
- New LucidMic logo and installer artwork.

The engine buffers 70 ms of audio, with additional latency possible in your audio
devices and call app. Other voices and music vocals may remain. Intel Macs are not supported.

## Downloads and source

- **LucidMic-1.0.0.dmg** — the app and driver. This is the file most people need.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — verify downloads with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`
  after downloading all three listed artifacts.
- **BlackHole-0.7.1-source.tar.gz** — exact source for the bundled GPL driver.
- **LucidMic-1.0.0-runtime-source.tar.gz** — pinned source for the inference runtime
  and its embedded dependencies, including eSpeak NG.
- The separate MIT-licensed [ONNX Runtime v1.28.2 source](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/archive/refs/tags/v1.28.2.tar.gz)
  is hosted upstream. Its build instructions are included in the runtime source bundle.
- GitHub's **Source code** archives contain LucidMic and the driver customization/build scripts.

LucidMic is GPL-3.0. All dependency licenses are bundled with the app.
